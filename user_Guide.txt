SmartScan — User Guide
1. Scan a Document

Open SmartScan.

Scan a document with the camera or import an existing image.

Add more pages if the document has multiple pages.

Reorder or remove pages as needed.

2. Edit a Page

Open a page to access the editing tools.

Crop — select the area you want to keep, then tap Apply.

After applying a crop, the cropped result remains visible in the editor.

Tap Use Page when you are finished editing.

Rotate — correct the page orientation.

Filters — adjust the page appearance.

You can reopen a page later and edit it again.

3. OCR — Recognize Text

Select OCR on a page to convert printed text into selectable text.

Tap OCR.

Wait while the text is recognized.

Review the recognized text.

Tap Copy Text to copy it.

OCR works locally on your device.

If you edit the page afterward, run OCR again to update the recognized text.

4. Save & Reopen

Your documents and page edits are saved locally.

You can leave a document and reopen it later. Saved edits and OCR text remain available.

5. Export

Depending on your needs, export your document as:

JPG — image format.

PNG — image format.

PDF — convenient for documents.

ZIP — useful for exporting multiple page images together.

After exporting, use the normal device download or share options.

6. Privacy

SmartScan is designed for local use.

Your scanned documents and OCR text are processed and stored on your device rather than uploaded to a SmartScan server.

7. Simple Workflow

Scan → Edit → OCR if needed → Save → Export / Share

SmartScan is intentionally simple: scan what you need, make the corrections you need, and keep your documents under your control.
