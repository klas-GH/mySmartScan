SmartScan — Quick Guide v1.10.8

Complete loop: Scan → Edit → OCR (optional) → Organize → Export / Share
Everything runs on this device. No account. No cloud.

1. Scan a Document

• Tap Scan document.
• Use the live camera shutter, or import images from your device.
• Add more pages, reorder, or remove them as needed.

2. Edit a Page

• Open a page for crop, rotate, and filters (Original, Grayscale, B&W, Enhance).
• Tap Use page when finished. Edits are lossless — the original is kept.
• You can reopen a page later and edit it again.

3. OCR — Recognize Text

• OCR is available on saved pages (from the document page list), not while still in the editor before Use page.
• Tap OCR on a page, or OCR all pages on the document.
• Review text, Copy text, or Run again after edits (editing a page clears old OCR).
• Badges show which pages already have OCR.
• Search Documents by name or OCR text — opening a hit jumps to the matched page.
• First OCR run downloads the engine and language data (one-time); then it works offline for that language.
• OCR runs locally on your device.

4. Organize

• Create folders and move documents into them.
• On Documents, use the checkboxes to select several and Move them in bulk.

5. Save and Reopen

• Documents, edits, and OCR text stay on this device.
• Leave and reopen anytime.

6. Export and Share

• PDF, JPG, or PNG. Multi-page JPG/PNG export as a ZIP.
• Share via the system share sheet when available, otherwise download.
• Very large documents may show a soft warning before export.

7. Privacy & install

• No account, no SmartScan server upload — data stays on this device.
• Install as an app (PWA) for quick access; the app shell works offline.

SmartScan is intentionally simple: scan what you need, correct what you need, keep control of your documents.
