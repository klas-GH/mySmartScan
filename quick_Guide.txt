SmartScan — Quick Guide

SmartScan is a fast, local-first document scanner. All documents and data stay on your device.

SCAN
• Tap the large circle to capture a page with the camera.
• Tap the ▧ button (left of capture) to import existing images from your gallery/files — adds to the current document or creates a new one.
• Add multiple pages; each appears in the scan session with "Add another" / "Done".

EDIT
Open any page to edit:
• Crop — drag corners to remove unwanted areas.
• Rotate — 90° increments.
• Filters — Original, Grayscale, Black & White, Enhance.
• Edits are non-destructive; original image is preserved, re-edit anytime.

OCR
• Tap OCR on any page to recognize text locally via Tesseract.js.
• 11 languages: English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese, Korean, Russian, Arabic.
• Settings → OCR language / Preprocessing (adaptive / grayscale / none) / Page segmentation (auto / single block / single line / word / raw line).
• Recognized text saved with the page; copy to clipboard.
• Re-run OCR after changing crop, rotation, or filter.

ORGANIZE
• Folders: create, rename, delete. Move documents via document menu (⋮).
• Documents with zero pages are automatically removed from views.

EXPORT & SHARE
• Per document: PDF, JPG, PNG, or ZIP (multi-page images).
• Settings → default export format.
• Native share sheet when supported; otherwise downloads.

SETTINGS
• Theme: System / Light / Dark.
• Default scan filter & export format.
• OCR language, preprocessing, page segmentation.

PRIVACY
• Documents and app data stay on this device. No account or cloud storage.
• Tesseract.js loaded from CDN for OCR; app shell works offline.

WORKFLOW
Scan / Import → Edit → OCR (optional) → Export / Share

SmartScan is intentionally small, local, and practical.
