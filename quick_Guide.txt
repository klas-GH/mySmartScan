SmartScan — Quick Guide

SmartScan is a simple, local document scanner designed to keep your documents on your device.

Scan

Use the camera to scan a document.

Add multiple pages to the same document.

Import existing images when needed.

Edit

Open any page to edit it:

Crop — remove unwanted areas.

Rotate — correct the page orientation.

Filters — improve the appearance of the scan.

Changes are non-destructive, so you can reopen and edit the page again.

OCR

Select OCR on a page to recognize printed text.

OCR runs locally on your device.

Recognized text can be selected and copied.

OCR results are saved with the page.

After changing the page's crop, rotation, or filter, you can run OCR again to refresh the text.

Export & Share

Export your documents as:

JPG

PNG

PDF

ZIP for multi-page image collections

You can also use your device's normal download/share options.

Privacy

SmartScan is designed to work locally.

Your scanned documents and OCR text are processed and stored locally rather than being uploaded to a SmartScan server.

Simple workflow

Scan → Edit → OCR if needed → Export / Share

That's it. SmartScan is intentionally kept small, local, and practical.
